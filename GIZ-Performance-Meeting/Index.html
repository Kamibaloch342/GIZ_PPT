SID

Technical Architecture

Session ID
The Verification Chain

The single most important innovation of Phase II. A robust tracking chain that ensures structural compliance and accurate location verification.

How the Lock Chain Works

1

Trainer Marks Attendance

App requires GPS fix. If trainer is not physically at the venue, location cannot be acquired and attendance creation is blocked.

→
2

Session ID Generated

A unique Session ID is generated — one per trainer per day. The ID is tied to the GPS coordinates captured at step 1. Cannot be generated offsite.

→
3

Dashboard Alert Fires

The Session ID immediately appears on the provincial coordinator's dashboard. Venue coordinates go live. Anyone can now visit that exact location without notifying the trainer.

→
4

IC Form Gated by Session ID

Implementation Coordinator cannot submit their monitoring form without entering the Session ID. The IC must physically visit the venue, obtain the ID from the trainer, and complete the form.

Monitoring Hierarchy

Trainer

👩‍🏫

Generates Session ID · Marks attendance · Teaches sequentially

IC (Physical)

🏃

Visits venue · Gets Session ID · Files IC form · Confirms session validity

Remote Coordinators

📞

Call sample of beneficiaries to confirm training is live · Report anomalies

QA Team (Stealth)

🔍

Can visit ANY location unannounced · Not coordinated with ICs or PCs · Files QA form independently

Eliminating Informal Channels

Previously, coordination happened over WhatsApp — trainers were alerted before monitoring visits, attendance could be fabricated. The Session ID system eliminated all informal channels by creating a centralized, real-time, tamper-evident coordination layer that every stakeholder was locked into.

TF

Field Technology

The Teaching Form
4 Hours · Sequential · Geo-Fenced

The teaching form is not a checkbox — it is a live enforcement engine. Once opened, it tracks time, location, and curriculum progression across the full session.

How the Form Works

📷

Session Start Photo

Trainer captures the room at the moment they begin. Timestamp + coordinates recorded.

⏱

4-Hour Sequential Timer

Topics appear one at a time in curriculum order on Day 1, then Day 2 topics on Day 2. Trainer cannot skip topics. Duration lock enforces minimum time per topic.

📍

Millisecond GPS Polling

App records coordinates at every millisecond. If trainer leaves the 1 km radius of the venue, the form immediately stops — data not saved. "It keeps crashing" — this is by design.

🎙

30% Audio Audit Sampling

1,930 sessions reviewed out of ~6,433 total. Abusive language detection active. Flagged for supervisor review.

📷

Session End Photo

Second photo taken at close. QA team cross-matches start vs. end photos to verify beneficiary presence for at least 4 hours.

01

Month 1

Institutional Transition
& Structural Redesign

Strategic Focus

  • Transition from Phase I evaluation to Phase II implementation
  • Monitoring architecture development
  • Governance redesign
  • Trainer pool assessment

The primary effort was devoted to reengineering the operational framework inherited from Phase I. While Phase I provided deep exposure to systemic weaknesses—particularly in monitoring transparency, data authenticity, and field-level governance—it lacked enforcement mechanisms.

The first institutional task was to analyze gaps observed during impact evaluation and convert them into system-level design requirements. Key deficiencies identified included absence of geo-verified monitoring, manual data entry, inflated Day-1 attendance, and negligible Day-2 retention.

Simultaneously, BISP provided a pool of Phase I trainers. Given a beneficiary target of approx 250,000 and a capacity of 1,056 per trainer, the required workforce was 237 trainers. A structured merit-based selection framework was introduced to ensure quality deployment.

Deficiencies → Governance Redesign

Key Challenge

Transitioning from an evaluation mindset to execution governance required immediate structural clarity.

Resolution

Designed a hierarchical governance model (ICs, Provincial Coordinators, Remote Monitoring Unit) creating a defined chain of accountability.

OCR

Technical Innovation

Solving the CNIC
Verification Bottleneck

As Day-2 CNIC photos flooded in from 224 trainers, manual review became impossible. We built a custom automated OCR pipeline to verify identity at scale.

The Bottleneck

Day-2 beneficiary CNICs were uploaded as raw photos from the field. With 68,000+ records, manual cross-verification was not scalable. Images varied in quality — blurry, rotated, dark, low-res.

OCR Pipeline — 5 Image Variations

1

Original

Baseline pass on unmodified image

2

Grayscale + High Contrast (3×)

Enhances faded or low-contrast cards

3

Upscaled 2× + Sharpened

Fixes small or distant CNIC photos

4

Binarized (B&W Threshold)

Strips patterned backgrounds

5

Inverted Colors

Handles white text on dark cards

Verification Logic Flow

Download CNIC Photo

Authenticated bulk download from SurveyCTO with 4 parallel threads.

Apple Vision OCR (5 passes)

macOS Vision framework runs on each image variation. Common OCR errors auto-corrected (O→0, I→1, B→8, S→5).

Fuzzy CNIC Matching

Sliding-window search across extracted digits. Best 13-digit sequence matched against registered CNIC using similarity ratio.

Age Validation

Birth year extracted (1964–2007 accepted range). Mismatches flagged for manual review.

Result Written to Live CSV

100% match → Auto-verified. 90–99% → Manual check. Below 90% → Rejected/Review.
